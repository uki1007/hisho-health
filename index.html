hisho-health

hisho-health は、開発者本人が自分の Google Health（Fitbit）のデータ（睡眠・心拍など）を、自分のパソコンで読むための個人用ツールです。一般の方への提供はしていません。

hisho-health is a personal tool that lets its developer read their own Google Health (Fitbit) data, such as sleep and heart rate, on their own computer. It is not offered to the public.
